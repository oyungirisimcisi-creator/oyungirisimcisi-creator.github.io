Nasıl oynanır?

Taşı kirişteki altı yuvadan birine koy. Her taşın etkisi değeri ile yuvanın çarpanının (×1, ×2, ×3) çarpımı kadardır. Sol ve sağ taraf arasındaki fark 10'a ulaşırsa tahterevalli devrilir. Ayrıntılar oyunun ana menüsündeki Kurallar bölümünde.

Rekorum kayboldu

How do I play?

Put the stone in one of the six slots on the beam. Each stone pushes with its value times the slot multiplier (×1, ×2, ×3). If the difference between the left and right side reaches 10, the seesaw tips over. See Rules in the game's main menu for details.

My best score is gone

Your best score is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it too.

Ads

The game is free and supported by ads. Watching an ad to keep playing when a game ends is completely optional. In Europe you can change your ad consent under Privacy settings in the main menu. On iPhone you can manage tracking permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
